# ນັບເລກກັບໝີນ້ອຍ · นับเลขกับหมีน้อย

A 3D maths game for young children (around 6 years old), in Lao or Thai.

**Play:** https://sanyapanyasa.github.io/nab-lex/

- Walk by dragging a finger (or the arrow keys) and punch 👊 the number bug that holds the right answer.
- Beat the boss by hitting the crystal with the right answer, then rescue the dino egg.
- Counting to 20, making 5 and 10, comparing with > < =, adding and subtracting within 20.
- Open **ฝึกกับผู้ปกครอง** / **ຝຶກກັບຜູ້ປົກຄອງ** to teach filling to a total or subtraction. Move stars to work through the first three questions, then try two questions from memory with pictures available on demand. After each answer, join or separate the same stars to see how addition and subtraction are related.
- The difficulty follows the child, the bear teaches after a wrong answer, and missed questions come back later.
- Install it from the browser (Add to Home Screen) to get an icon, full screen and offline play.
- Progress is saved only on the device. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere.
