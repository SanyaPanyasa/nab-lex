# ນັບເລກກັບໝີນ້ອຍ · นับเลขกับหมีน้อย

A maths game for young children (around 6 years old), in Lao or Thai.

**Play short maths rounds:** https://sanyapanyasa.github.io/nab-lex/

**Open the 3D adventure:** https://sanyapanyasa.github.io/nab-lex/?mode=adventure

**Open the practice lessons directly:** https://sanyapanyasa.github.io/nab-lex/?mode=practice

**Textbook lessons, chapter by chapter (Lao Grade 1 maths):** https://sanyapanyasa.github.io/nab-lex/lessons.html

The main game uses one hands-on question and two follow-up questions per round. Pair frogs, add dinosaurs, take away turtles, or fill a missing part. Each topic links to its Grade 1 textbook chapter.

In the 3D adventure:

- Walk by dragging a finger (or the arrow keys) and punch 👊 the number bug that holds the right answer.
- Beat the boss by hitting the crystal with the right answer, then rescue the dino egg.
- Counting to 20, making 5 and 10, comparing with > < =, adding and subtracting within 20.
- The difficulty follows the child, the bear teaches after a wrong answer, and missed questions come back later.

In practice with a grown-up:

- Choose a three-question round with animal counters, drawing, and a chance to think independently, or a five-question round.
- Each tap on an animal or empty space moves a companion one step on the number line. Children can see both the current amount and how many they have added or removed. [Try one guided question, 2 + □ = 7](https://sanyapanyasa.github.io/nab-lex/?mode=practice&example=2-to-7).
- During guided practice, a hand points to the next counter. For missing parts, the added animals are numbered when the child finishes counting. In comparisons the child can tap the side with more frogs or choose equal, then read the completed comparison. Parents can open the teaching tip below each question.
- After an answer, continue, finish early, or open the explanation. Join and separate the same animals to connect addition and subtraction, or swap the left and right groups to see what happens to a comparison.
- Each practice skill has its own companion. It grows through three forms as the child answers independent questions correctly on the first try.

Install it from the browser for full-screen and offline play. Once the 3D adventure has loaded, it can also be played offline. Progress is saved on the device.
